Brief SEO Content

Transport management software for colleges, schools, and campus operations.

iBoss EMS provides a college transport management system for route master, vehicle master, route-vehicle mapping, student bus route mapping, staff bus route mapping, trip sheets, kilometer usage, student Excel upload, employee upload, transport fee collection, and online tracking of students and vehicles.

The module connects transport operations with student records, employee records, fee collection, route allocation, and management reports. Institutions can reduce manual route planning, improve fee visibility, and monitor vehicle movement with better operational control.

Key Features

  • Route master and route stop management
  • Vehicle master with fleet and document records
  • Route - vehicle mapping for daily operations
  • Student and staff mapping with bus route
  • Trip sheet and kilometer usage tracking
  • Student upload Excel and employee upload
  • Transport fee collection and payment visibility
  • Online tracking of student and vehicle movement

For Transport Teams

Manage routes, vehicles, student and staff allocations, trip sheets, kilometer usage, Excel uploads, and daily fleet operations.

For Finance Teams

Track route-wise transport fee collection, student transport fee status, pending dues, payment history, and collection reports.

For Parents and Management

View student transport mapping, route information, vehicle status, and online tracking reports for safer campus transport visibility.

Route to Real-Time Tracking

Connect route planning, bus mapping, fee collection, and live transport visibility.

With iBoss EMS, institutions can maintain transport master data, map students and staff to routes, monitor kilometer usage, collect transport fees, and track student or vehicle movement online from one connected platform.
